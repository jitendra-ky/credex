## Who Is the Exact Target User

Not "startups." The specific user is:

**An engineering manager or CTO at a seed-to-Series-A B2B SaaS startup, 5–30 person engineering team, currently paying for 2–4 AI tools with at least $300/mo in combined spend.**

They made their current tooling decisions 6–12 months ago when headcount was smaller, without a deliberate procurement process. They signed up for what they needed at the time, added tools as the team grew, never cancelled the old ones, and have never run a systematic comparison. They are not stupid — they are busy. They have a renewal coming up, or their CFO just asked "what's this $2,400/year Cursor line item," and now they're paying attention.

They know they're probably overspending. They don't have time to research every vendor's pricing matrix. They'd love someone to do it for them. This tool does exactly that.

**Secondary user:** Finance/ops lead at the same company — often the one who actually noticed the line item and is trying to justify or cut it before the board meeting.

## How to Get the First 100 Users in 30 Days with $0

1. **Day 1–3: Launch on Hacker News.** Post "Show HN: Free AI spend auditor for startups — paste your tools, get savings breakdown." Write the text like you're explaining it to an engineer, not pitching it. No marketing language. Target Monday morning US time (9am PT). HN Show HN posts with real tool demos that are immediately usable get 50–200 upvotes. 100 users from one post is achievable.

2. **Day 4–7: Post to 3 specific Slack communities.** In each, share a real anonymised audit result (screenshots of a high-savings case). Ask for feedback on the recommendation quality. Slack communities respond to "help me improve this" better than pure promotion. Target: Ramen Club, Indie Hackers community Slack, SaaStr Slack.

3. **Day 7–14: Find 10 Twitter/X threads where founders or EMs are complaining about AI tool costs.** Reply with "I built a tool for exactly this" + link. Not a mass reply — find threads where the person is actively asking for help or venting. 10 targeted replies > 1 broadcast tweet.

4. **Day 14–21: Reach out directly to 20 YC-backed startups from S24/W25 batch.** Find engineering team leads on LinkedIn. Message: "I built a free AI spend audit tool — interested in a quick audit of your stack? No strings attached." Conversion rate should be ~30–40% just for the free audit.

5. **Day 21–30: Partner with one newsletter.** Offer to write a free "Is your startup overspending on AI tools?" guest post for a developer-focused newsletter (TLDR, Bytes, Pointer). Include a link to the tool. Cost: free. Upside: 500–2,000 opens.
