## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**  
A: Yes, completely free. No credit card, no login. You see the full audit before we ask for your email. The catch is honest: if your audit shows more than $500/month in savings potential, we'll let you know that Credex can help you capture even more of that savings through discounted AI credits. You decide whether that's relevant to you.

**Q: What AI tools does the audit cover?**  
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude (GUI + API), ChatGPT (GUI + API), Anthropic API direct, OpenAI API direct, Gemini, and v0/Vercel. These cover the vast majority of AI spend at seed-to-Series-A startups. If you use a tool not on this list, note it in the feedback field — we're adding tools based on demand.

**Q: How do you know your pricing data is accurate?**  
A: Every price in our audit engine is cited to the vendor's official pricing page with the date we verified it. We update prices as we spot changes. You can see the full `PRICING_DATA.md` file in the [public GitHub repo](https://github.com/jitendra-ky/credex). If you spot an error, open a PR.

**Q: Is my data private? Do you store what I enter?**  
A: Your audit is stored securely in our database. If you choose to share your audit via the public link, identifying details (email, company name) are stripped from the public version — only tool names and savings numbers are shown. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties.

**Q: What is Credex and why is it offering this for free?**  
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that overforecast their usage. The audit tool is genuinely free and useful on its own. Where it benefits Credex: users with significant savings potential are often good candidates for Credex credits, which can save them even more than the plan changes the audit recommends. We only surface this for high-savings cases, not as a default upsell.
